TriPoint Diagnostics - Expansion Plan
Servicing and maintenance for Mercedes vans, plus commercial van tuning focused on work-use benefits
This document sets out a practical expansion plan for two new revenue streams under the TriPoint 
Diagnostics brand: (1) Mercedes van servicing and brake work, and (2) van tuning focused on load-
carrying drivability, torque delivery, and route efficiency rather than generic 'more power' 
marketing. The goal is to add repeatable work, improve customer lifetime value, and create a broader 
offer stack without diluting the diagnostics-first identity of the business.

2. Expansion A - Mercedes van servicing and brake work
Proposed focus: scheduled servicing, inspection-led maintenance, and brake work for Mercedes vans 
only. Target models: Vito W447, Sprinter W906 and W907/W910, and Citan W415/W420.
2.1 Positioning

Mobile Mercedes van servicing for owner-drivers, sole traders, and small fleets.

Planned maintenance done at home, yard, or workplace - built around convenience and less 
downtime.

A premium independent alternative to dealer servicing for routine maintenance jobs.

Separate this offer from diagnostics in the menu, but use diagnostics as the trust anchor.
2.2 Best jobs to offer

Minor (A-style) oil service packages.

Major (B-style) service packages once the checklist is fully standardised.

Front brake pads and front pads plus discs packages.

Rear brake work only with strong quoting rules and condition qualifiers.

Service plus visual vehicle health check as an upsell.
2.3 Jobs to avoid or tightly control

Anything needing ramp-level access or extensive underbody stripping.

2.5 Why it is commercially attractive

Creates annual and semi-annual repeat work instead of relying only on urgent faults.

Lower cognitive load than complex diagnostics, so it helps fill planned slots in the diary.

Strong cross-sell potential from existing Mercedes van diagnostics customers.

Makes your business more 'sticky' because customers who trust you with servicing are more likely 
to return for diagnostics and repairs.
2.6 Recommended advertising funnel



service, and matching brake pages.

Use fixed 'from' pricing and clear inclusions so customers self-qualify before contacting you.

Primary acquisition channels: Google Business Profile services, existing customer upsell, reminder 
campaigns, local SEO pages, then tightly-targeted Google Ads.

Best initial PPC themes: 'Mercedes van service', 'Sprinter service', 'Vito service', 'Sprinter brake 
pads and discs'.

3. Expansion B - Commercial van tuning
This expansion should be positioned as a work-van optimisation service, not a boy-racer tuning 
product. The strongest commercial message is improved real-world drivability for loaded vans: 
stronger mid-range pull, easier overtakes, better hill performance, less frequent downshifts, and 
smoother daily use.
3.1 Positioning

Lead with 'load and driveability', not headline bhp claims.

Talk about usable torque, route performance, and driving feel under payload.

Use Stage 1 remap as the technical descriptor, not the front-end sales headline.

Keep this section separate from diagnostics and separate again from any future 
enthusiast/performance work.

3.5 Qualification process

Collect reg, make/model, engine, gearbox, mileage, typical load, route type, and any current faults 
before quoting.

Build a mandatory pre-check or diagnostic screen before any tune is sold.

Do not promise fixed mpg gains; discuss potential economy benefits depending on route, load, and 
driving style.

Add a handover note reminding customers to check insurance or fleet policy requirements for 
modifications.
3.6 Best advertising funnel

Do not lead with generic 'stage 1 remap' messaging.

Lead with work-use outcomes: pulls better when loaded, stronger mid-range torque, better hill 
performance, smoother on route.

Build dedicated pages: Van Load and Driveability Tune, Van Economy Tune, Fleet Van Tuning.

Use a quote-first CTA: reg + engine + load/use pattern + postcode.

Best PPC themes: 'van economy tune', 'van torque tune', 'transit custom economy remap', 'sprinter 
torque tune', 'fleet van tuning'.

Keep tuning campaigns separate from diagnostics campaigns to avoid polluting search terms and 
audience intent.
3.7 Why it is commercially attractive

Higher ticket than routine servicing.

Broadens your market beyond Mercedes while still staying van-first.

Strong upsell from existing van customers who already trust your technical ability.

Easy to frame around business outcomes rather than vanity performance.
4. Combined rollout strategy
4.1 First 30 days

Launch Mercedes van servicing as the first expansion.

Publish a simple fixed-price menu for minor service, major service, and front brake packages.

Add these offers to the website, GBP services, and WhatsApp quick-reply templates.

Build a reminder workflow for existing customers: annual service reminders and brake checks.
4.2 Days 31-60

Track time-on-job, parts margin, and conversion rates on service/brake enquiries.

Add rear brake jobs only if your quoting process and contingency surcharges are working.

Start SEO pages for model-specific service and brake offers.
4.3 Days 61-90

Launch van tuning with a diagnostics/pre-check gate.

Publish three tuning pages: load and driveability, economy, and fleet tuning.

Run tuning as a separate ad funnel and separate offer family.


Cross-sell tuning only to suitable vehicles and established customers first.
4.4 Priority recommendation
If only one expansion is launched immediately, choose servicing first. It offers more predictable 
scheduling, easier customer education, and better repeat revenue. Tuning should follow once your 
admin, quoting, and qualification process are mature enough to support it without distracting from 
diagnostics.
5. Simple profit logic

Servicing makes sense because it fills quieter planned slots and improves retention.

Brake work makes sense because packaged front brake jobs are easy to understand and easy to 
sell.

Tuning makes sense because it is a higher-ticket add-on with strong business-use messaging when 
sold correctly.

Diagnostics should remain the trust anchor and the premium front-end offer.

The biggest risk is mixing too many audiences in one funnel. Keep diagnostics, servicing, and 
tuning distinct in your messaging and advertising.
